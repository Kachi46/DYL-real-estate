Frequently asked questions

A quick guide to listings, verification, inspections and accounts.

What does verified mean?

It means DYL reviewed the title information submitted for that listing. It does not replace independent legal, survey or registry due diligence.

Can I inspect a property from the site?

Yes. Open a property, choose Book an inspection, select a future date and time, and submit your contact details. The request is pending until the team confirms it.

Can I post a property?

Yes. Create an account, open My Dashboard, add your property details, images and location coordinates, then submit it for review.

Are all listings in Enugu?

The current catalog is focused on Enugu. Sellers may add other Nigerian locations where the listing is genuinely located.

How do I report a concern?

Use the Trust and legal page to email or WhatsApp the review team before sending money.
