About DYL

Property decisions built on better information.

DYL Real-Estate Services is a title-focused property platform helping buyers, renters, landlords, agents and developers connect around Enugu and across Nigeria.

Our mission

We make property discovery clearer by showing what has been submitted, what has been reviewed, and what still needs attention. Our team combines practical listing support with a visible verification process.

Why clients use DYL

Clarity

See property details, location, price and verification status in one place.

Local focus

Find property and inspection opportunities in Enugu neighborhoods and beyond.

Human support

Ask questions, request an inspection, or contact the property desk directly.

Our promise

A verified badge describes the documents reviewed by DYL. It is not a legal guarantee, so we encourage independent legal, survey and registry checks before payment.
