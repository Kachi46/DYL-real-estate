DYL Real-Estate Services helps people find property in Enugu and across Nigeria. Verification is a review signal, not a substitute for your own legal and physical due diligence.

How verification works

Every listing submitted for review is checked by our team against the title document and seller information provided. A verified badge means the submitted documents passed our review process. It does not guarantee ownership, planning approval, market value, or that a transaction is risk-free.

1. Documents

The seller submits the available title information.

2. Review

Our team checks names, dates, plot details and records where available.

3. Decision

Listings are marked verified, pending, or rejected so the status stays visible.

Before you pay

  • Inspect the property in person with a qualified surveyor or professional.
  • Use an independent property lawyer to confirm title, encumbrances and required consents.
  • Confirm the seller's identity and ensure payment details match the contracting party.
  • Do not pay because a listing is marked verified; keep copies of every document and receipt.

Found inaccurate information, suspicious documents, or a listing that should not be live? Contact the review team before sending money.
