Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. What we collect

When you register, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and chosen account type (User, Landlord, Agent, or Developer). If you post a listing, we also collect the property details and any title documents you upload for verification. If you contact a seller through the platform, we collect the message and contact details you provide.

2. Email preferences

During registration you can choose whether to receive property listing updates, real estate insights, and news about new project developments. This choice is saved to your account and you can change it at any time by contacting us or updating your account settings.

3. How we use your information

Your information is used to operate your account, display your listings (if you post any), connect you with sellers or buyers you choose to contact, and — only if you've opted in — send you email updates. We don't sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Title documents

Title documents uploaded for property verification are reviewed by our admin team to assess a listing's verification status. These documents are kept only as long as needed for that purpose and are not shared publicly.

5. Data security

Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing and are never stored or transmitted in plain text. We use standard security practices to protect your data, though no online platform can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices

You can update your account details, change your email preferences, or request account deletion at any time by contacting us through the details in the site footer.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the platform after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
